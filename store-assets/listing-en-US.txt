TITLE
Study Cafe: Pomodoro Timer

SHORT
Pomodoro focus timer & study timer. Earn points, keep streaks, grow your cafe.

FULL
Study Cafe is a free Pomodoro timer and study timer for Android. Focus for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break, and earn points that grow your own virtual cafe. It is made for students, remote workers and anyone who wants to stay focused and build a daily study habit.

WHAT IS THE POMODORO TECHNIQUE?
The Pomodoro technique splits work into short focus sessions with breaks in between. In Study Cafe a focus session lasts 25 minutes, a short break lasts 5 minutes, and after every 4 focus sessions you get a 15-minute long break. The timer switches between them for you.

WHY STUDY CAFE?
- Focus timer with automatic focus, short break and long break sessions
- Points for every finished session, spent on items that decorate your virtual cafe
- Daily streaks and achievements that reward consistent studying
- Calming ambient sounds while you study: rain, fireplace and brown noise
- Statistics: see your study time by week, month and all time
- Optional leaderboard: sign in with email or Google to compare points with other students
- Session-end reminders, even when the app is in the background
- Share your progress as a card with friends
- Available in English and Turkish

QUICK ANSWERS
Is Study Cafe free? Yes. It is free and supported by ads. An optional rewarded ad gives bonus points.
Do I need an account? No. The timer, cafe, streaks, sounds and statistics work without one. An account is only needed for the leaderboard.
Who is it for? Students preparing for exams, people doing deep work, and anyone who wants a simple focus timer for studying or working.
Which languages are supported? English and Turkish.
How do I delete my account? Open Profile > Delete Account, or follow https://cynorra.github.io/studycafe/delete-account.html
